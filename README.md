# Excel-Sales-Dashboard-Project
The dashboard highlights key performance indicators (KPIs), including Total Sales, Total Gross Profit, Gross Profit Margin. It also provides insights into Monthly Revenue & Profit, Region wise comparison, Top 5 countries by Revenue. 
By transforming raw sales data into meaningful visual insights, this project helps executives identify growth opportunities, evaluate business performance, monitor profitability, and make informed strategic decisions

Key Features
Executive Overview: High-level snapshot of overall business performance.
Sales Performance Analysis: Tracks total sales and identifies sales trends over time.
Profitability Analysis: Evaluates total profit and profit margins to understand business performance.
Product Performance: Identifies top-performing countries by Revenue. 
Interactive Dashboard: Enables users to explore business insights through dynamic filters and visualizations.

Project Objective
The primary objective of this dashboard is to simplify complex sales data into actionable business insights, helping executives and decision-makers assess financial performance, identify profitable opportunities, and support strategic business planning.
